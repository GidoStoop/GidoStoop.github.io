Not Just Aesthetic

The Dimension Trap

  • Using 2-dimensions to represent 1-dimensional change
  • Or even using 3-dimensions…
  • Lie factor: 59.4
  • These types of visualization should be avoided!

6 Rules of Graphical Integrity

01
Exact Proportionality
Visual surface must directly mirror data values
02
Thorough Labeling
Explicit notes defeat ambiguity and misinterpretation
03
Data Variation First
Highlight shifts in data, not shifts in design
04
Standardized Units
Use deflated currency and per-capita adjustments
05
Dimensional Parity
Keep visual dimensions equal to data dimensions
06
Contextual Completeness
Always show full ranges and comparisons

The ‘Duck’ Data Visualization

  • When a graph is more design than data, it may be called ‘duck’. Referring to the ‘Big Duck’ building.
  • “It is all right to decorate construction, but never construct decoration”
  • Robert Venturi, Architect

Data-Ink Ratio

  • Data Ink: Lines, bars, dots, anything showing direct data
  • Total Ink: Gridlines, borders, shadows, background fills etc.
  • High Data-Ink ratio (0.9 – 1.0): Reports, financial charts
  • Moderate Data-Ink Ratio (0.5 – 0.89): Storytelling, presentation
  • Low Data-Ink Ratio (Below 0.5): Marketing, ads, promotion

Friendly vs. Unfriendly Visuals

  • Vertical rotated labels, cryptic codes, detached legends
  • Friendly: Horizontal text, clear annotation, direct in-place callouts, color coded

Sentences, Tables or Graphics?

  • Sentences: Best for 1 or 2 numbers embedded in narrative
  • Tables: Best for exact values, if lookups are important and small datasets (<20 numbers)
  • Graphics: Best for large datasets, complex structures
  • Tip: Graphic in body of report, table in appendix

Age of AI

  • Makes tools like Plotly, Matplotlib and ggplot2 more accessible.
  • Knowing plt.plot() is no longer enough to outcompete others
  • Data ordering and cleaning made easier
  • Structuring
  • Anomaly detection
  • Outlier flagging

Don’t Let AI Generate the Graphics Directly!

  • Fabricated numbers
  • Chartjunk
  • Overlapping labels
  • Rather, let AI generate the code. Prompt with concepts we discussed like
  • Data-ink ratio,
  • Zero baseline,
  • Lie factor
  • Direct labeling
  • And be skeptical
